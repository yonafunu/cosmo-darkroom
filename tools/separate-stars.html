Stars

Separate stars tool

Splits the image into two layers: the nebula without its stars, and the stars alone.

ExperimentalCOSMOStars, the in-house network of this tool, is experimental and not recommended for finished images: defects remain on some images. The tool can also run with RC-Astro StarXTerminator, from your own licensed installation.

How to use it

  1. Select the step in the sequence, or add the tool from Tools.
  2. Set it, then press Preview: the step is computed without being applied.
  3. Press Apply to keep the result, or change the settings and preview again.

Settings

Nebula / Stars
The layer shown: the nebula without its stars, or the stars alone.

When to use it

To process the nebula and the stars separately. The stars become a layer of their own, which the other tools can reach.

Image

Works on a linear or a stretched image.
