Dark structures tool

Deepens dust lanes, globules and dark nebulae.

How to use it

  1. Open Tools and click the tool: it is added after the selected step.
  2. Move its settings: the image follows as you move them (Live preview).
  3. Press Apply to keep the result. Before / after compares at any time.

Settings

Amount
How much deeper the dark structures get.
Layers
The widest dark structures that count: more layers reach larger dark nebulae.
Mask
Everywhere, Bright parts (the object, not the sky) or Dark parts. Stars are not in the mask.
Threshold
Where the mask starts, from the sky background (0 %) to the brightest part of the object (100 %).

When to use it

When dust lanes and dark nebulae look flat against the glowing background.

Image

Works only on a stretched image.
