Hα boost tool

Brings out the Hα regions: red minus its continuum.

How to use it

  1. Open Tools and click the tool: it is added after the selected step.
  2. Move its settings: the image follows as you move them (Live preview).
  3. Press Apply to keep the result. Before / after compares at any time.

Settings

Amount
How much of the Hα found is added to the image.
Continuum
How red the continuum is taken to be. More: only the reddest regions count as Hα.
Pink / Red
Pink: Hα goes to red and its share of Hβ to blue. Red: Hα goes to red only.
Mask
Everywhere, Bright parts (the object, not the sky) or Dark parts. Stars are not in the mask.
Threshold
Where the mask starts, from the sky background (0 %) to the brightest part of the object (100 %).

When to use it

On emission nebulae taken with a colour camera, to make the hydrogen regions stand out.

Image

Works only on a stretched image.
