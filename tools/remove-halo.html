Stars

Remove halo tool

Removes the halo of a bright star picked on the image.

How to use it

  1. Open Tools and click the tool: it is added after the selected step.
  2. Move its settings: the image follows as you move them (Live preview).
  3. Press Apply to keep the result. Before / after compares at any time.

Right-click the star on the image: its halo is measured and two circles are drawn, where the halo ends and where the star keeps its own glow.

Settings

Amount
How much of the halo is taken off.
Halo radius
Where the halo ends: the outer circle. Measured when the star is chosen.
Glow kept
The glow the star keeps: the inner, dashed circle. Less gives a tighter star.
Residue
Removes the veil left by overlapping halos of neighbouring stars.
No star at its centre
For what a removed star leaves on a starless image: a ring, a disc, a hollow.

When to use it

When a bright star keeps a ring or a glow. The star is picked with a right-click on the image.

Image

Works on a linear or a stretched image.
