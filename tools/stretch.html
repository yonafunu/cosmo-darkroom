Stretch

Stretch tool

Brings out the faint signal: generalised hyperbolic stretch (GHS), arcsinh or histogram, with the curve drawn over the histogram.

How to use it

  1. Open Tools and click the tool: it is added after the selected step.
  2. Move its settings: the image follows as you move them (Live preview).
  3. Press Apply to keep the result. Before / after compares at any time.

In GHS, right-click a region of the image: the stretch is centred on its level; keep the button down and drag up for a stronger stretch.

Settings

Histogram / GHS / Arcsinh
Histogram: black point, midtones and white point. GHS: generalised hyperbolic stretch. Arcsinh: a gentle stretch that keeps the colours of the stars.
Auto
The settings are computed from the image; any manual change takes over.
Stretch factor
How strong the stretch is.
Local intensity (b)
GHS: how concentrated the contrast is around the symmetry point. Negative values are gentle (−0.8 by default).
Per channel / Luminance
Per channel: stronger nebula colours, bright stars turn white. Luminance: the colours are kept.
Colour, Natural stars
Arcsinh: how much colour is kept, and how the stars fade to white towards their core.
Soft star cores
Arcsinh: melts the sharp edge of a bright star's white disc into its halo. Small stars and the nebula do not change: on the M 42 HDR stack, at 50 %, 0.1 % of the pixels are modified.
Linked / Unlinked
Histogram: one curve for the three colours, or one per colour.

When to use it

The turning point of the sequence: before it the image is linear, after it the image is the one you see. The tools of the stretched image come after.

Image

Made for a linear image; it can be used on a stretched one.
