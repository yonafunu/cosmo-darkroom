Qt and PySide6 (LGPL version 3). COSMO Darkroom uses these libraries as separate shared libraries, unmodified.
They are in the application's folder (macOS: COSMO Darkroom.app/Contents/Frameworks; Windows: the "_internal"
folder of the installation). You may replace them with your own compatible build of the same libraries. Their
source code is available from https://download.qt.io and https://pypi.org/project/PySide6/. The text of the
GNU LGPL version 3 is at https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.html.

Catalogue data. The Gaia DR3 catalogue queried by the Colour calibration and Analysis tools is provided by the
European Space Agency (ESA) mission Gaia (https://www.cosmos.esa.int/gaia), processed by the Gaia Data
Processing and Analysis Consortium (DPAC).
