Privacy

Data and privacy

Data handled by this site and by the software.

This site

It has no account, no form and no cookie. The fonts are those of your device; the images and the video are served with the pages.

Visits are counted with GoatCounter, an open-source audience measurement that sets no cookie and keeps no personal data. For each page viewed it receives the address of the page, the referring site, the browser, the screen size and the country deduced from the IP address; the IP address itself is not stored. Clicks on the download and support buttons are counted the same way. The statistics are visible only to the author of COSMO. Blocking the domains goatcounter.com and gc.zgo.at, as many ad blockers do, stops the counting.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, and the installers are downloaded from GitHub. Like any host, GitHub receives the technical data of a connection (IP address, page or file requested) in order to answer; see GitHub's privacy statement.

The software

All computation runs on your computer. Your images are not sent anywhere.

Two tools query the Gaia star catalogue of the European Space Agency, and only when you ask: colour calibration on Gaia, and the depth measurement of the Analysis window. Only the coordinates of the field are sent. The answer is kept on your disk: a field is requested only once.

At startup, at most once a day, COSMO asks GitHub for the number of the latest published version, to tell you when a newer one exists. Nothing about you or your images is sent; GitHub receives the technical data of the connection. Nothing is installed automatically. The check can be turned off: Help › Check for updates at startup.

GraXpert and the RC-Astro engines, if you use them, are separate software: their model downloads and licence checks follow their publishers' terms. COSMO stores no licence key.
